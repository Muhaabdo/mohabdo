What is included

Everything between the search and the sales call.

Account and campaign structure

Campaigns split by service, project and city, with keyword research in Arabic and English and negative keywords that keep spend away from irrelevant searches.

Ads and landing pages

Ad copy written for each intent, and landing pages built to match the ad, so the click turns into a call, a WhatsApp chat or a form.

Conversion tracking

Google Tag Manager and GA4 set up to track calls, WhatsApp, forms and thank-you pages, then imported into Google Ads so bidding learns from real actions.

Weekly optimization and reporting

Bids, budgets, search terms and ads reviewed every week against cost per lead and lead quality, with a report that compares Ads conversions to the leads your team received.

How I work

Five steps from first call to running campaigns.

  1. Discovery call

    Free call to understand the business, the margins and where leads are lost today.

  2. Audit or plan

    For an existing account, an audit of structure, tracking and wasted spend. For a new account, a campaign plan with budget split.

  3. Tracking first

    Tags, conversions and lead forms are set up and tested before any budget is spent.

  4. Launch

    Campaigns go live with tight targeting, then expand as data comes in.

  5. Optimize weekly

    Search terms, bids, ads and pages are improved every week against cost per qualified lead.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Google Ads management include?

Campaign structure, keyword research, ad copy, landing page recommendations or builds, conversion tracking with GTM and GA4, weekly optimization and a monthly report on cost per lead and lead quality.

How long before Google Ads brings leads?

A well-tracked search campaign usually brings its first leads in the first days. The first month is about learning which keywords, ads and pages produce qualified leads; costs normally improve from the second month as budget moves to what works.

Do you work with small budgets?

Yes, as long as the budget is concentrated. A small budget spread across many services and cities rarely works; the same budget on one service in one area often does.

Do I keep ownership of my Google Ads account?

Yes. The account stays in your name and I work through manager access. You keep the account, the data and the conversion history.

Which industries do you work with?

Mainly real estate, B2B and service businesses such as elevators and technical services, business setup and e-commerce.
