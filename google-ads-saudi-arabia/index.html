How the Saudi market behaves

Details that change results in Saudi campaigns.

  • Arabic and English queries side by side. The same buyer may search “elevator maintenance Riyadh” and “صيانة مصاعد الرياض”. Both need their own ad groups and ad copy.
  • City-level targeting. Riyadh, Jeddah and the Eastern Province behave like separate markets, with different competition and different service coverage. Budgets are split accordingly.
  • The weekend is Friday and Saturday. Ad schedules and call coverage follow the Saudi working week, not the Egyptian or European one.
  • WhatsApp is the main lead channel. Most service inquiries start as a WhatsApp chat, so the page puts WhatsApp first and every click is tracked.
  • Seasonality around Ramadan and Eid. Search volume and response times shift, and campaigns are adjusted before the season, not after.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does Google Ads cost in Saudi Arabia?

There is no fixed price. You pay Google directly for clicks, with a daily budget you set in Saudi riyals, plus a monthly management fee. Cost per click depends on the industry and the city. The practical approach is a focused first month on your most profitable service in one or two cities, then scaling what produces leads at an acceptable cost.

Can you run campaigns in Arabic?

Yes. Campaigns, ad copy and landing pages are written in Arabic, in English, or in both, depending on how your customers search.

Do you need to be based in Saudi Arabia to manage my account?

No. I work remotely from Cairo, which is one hour behind Riyadh, so working hours overlap almost completely. Meetings run on Google Meet or WhatsApp.

Can you build the website as well as the ads?

Yes. I build landing pages and full static websites, so the ads and the pages are planned together and tracked the same way.

How do you measure WhatsApp and phone leads?

Every WhatsApp click, call click and form submission is tracked through Google Tag Manager and GA4 and imported into Google Ads as a conversion. I also compare those numbers with the leads your team actually receives, because a click on WhatsApp is not always a qualified lead.
