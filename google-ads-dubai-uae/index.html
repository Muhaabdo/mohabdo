What is included

From account structure to the report your sales team trusts.

  • Campaign structure per project or service. Each project, area or setup type gets its own campaign so budget follows what sells.
  • Landing pages built for the offer. One page per project or service, fast on mobile, with WhatsApp, call and form all tracked.
  • Lead qualification in the form. Short questions on budget, timeline and nationality or residency filter weak leads before they reach your team.
  • Account hygiene. Advertiser verification, consistent business details and policy-safe ad copy, because real estate and financial-adjacent accounts are reviewed closely and suspensions cost weeks.
  • Leads vs conversions reporting. Google Ads counts a WhatsApp click as a conversion. I compare those numbers with the leads your CRM actually received, so decisions are made on real leads.

How the UAE market behaves

What changes when the buyer is in Dubai, or abroad.

  • Buyers are often outside the UAE. Property campaigns may target GCC, Europe or Asia as well as UAE residents, each with its own language, hours and expectations.
  • Competition on project names is high. Brokers and developers bid on the same project names, so landing page quality and message match decide the cost per lead.
  • Free zone vs mainland. Business setup searches split by structure and by emirate. Campaigns follow that split instead of mixing intents.
  • English-first, Arabic where it matters. Most UAE campaigns run in English, with Arabic versions for audiences that search in Arabic.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much should a Dubai real estate broker spend on Google Ads?

It depends on the projects and the target buyers. A project with high competition on its name costs more per click than a niche area. I recommend starting with one or two projects, measuring cost per qualified lead from your CRM, and moving budget to what produces viewings and deals.

Why do real estate Google Ads accounts get suspended?

Common causes are unverified advertiser details, business information that does not match across the account, website and ads, and ad copy or pages that break Google's policies. I set up and review accounts to reduce that risk from the start.

Do you only work with real estate in the UAE?

Real estate and business setup are where I have UAE experience. I also run Google Ads for B2B and service companies in Saudi Arabia and Egypt.

Are you based in Dubai?

No. I work remotely from Cairo, two hours behind Dubai, and meet clients on Google Meet or WhatsApp.
